About

Dimitris I. Anastasiou is Assistant Professor of Corporate Finance in the Department of Business Administration at the Athens University of Economics and Business (AUEB). His research is empirical and spans banking, corporate finance, behavioural finance and SME finance. He asks how sentiment, uncertainty and risk shape the choices of depositors, borrowers and banks.

His articles appear in journals including Financial Management, the Journal of Financial Stability, Risk Analysis, the Journal of International Money and Finance, the Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization, Energy Economics and Economics Letters, with further work forthcoming in the International Journal of Central Banking. He serves on the editorial board of Financial Economics Letters and referees for several journals. In the RePEc/IDEAS ranking of economists based on the last ten years of publications, he is among the top 7% of registered authors (September 2026).

He holds a B.Sc. in Economics, an M.Sc. in Applied Economics and Finance and a Ph.D. in Finance, all from AUEB. Before joining the faculty he was Senior Research Economist at Alpha Bank, where he analysed macroeconomic trends and produced forecasts, and he taught at AUEB as an adjunct lecturer. Earlier he worked as a research assistant at AbbVie on healthcare-outcome projects.

He loves God and believes that scientific advances are proof of His glory.
